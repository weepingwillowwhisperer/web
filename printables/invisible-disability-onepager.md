# Invisible Disabilities: A Quick Guide to Care (Print-Friendly)

**Purpose:** a one-page checklist for friends/teams supporting someone whose symptoms are mostly invisible (fatigue, pain, brain fog, sensory overload). Not medical advice.

## What to assume (even if you don't see it)
- Symptoms fluctuate daily; energy is a budget.
- "Looking fine" is not data.
- Silence often means coping.

## Language swaps
| Instead of… | Try… |
| --- | --- |
| "You're just tired." | "How's your energy today?" |
| "You were fine yesterday." | "What do you need right now?" |
| "Just push through." | "What can we postpone?" |

## One-minute support checklist
- [ ] Ask: "Do you want solutions, help, or just company?"
- [ ] Offer clear choices (A/B).
- [ ] Lower sensory load: dim light, reduce noise, fewer notifications.
- [ ] Give time buffer; no surprise timing changes.
- [ ] Normalize rest without guilt.

## Red flags (when to urge medical attention)
- [ ] Sudden new weakness, vision change, or severe dizziness
- [ ] Chest pain, shortness of breath, or fainting
- [ ] Confusion that worsens or extreme headache

If you're unsure and it feels urgent, call emergency services.
