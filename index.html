Guide

Start with intention, then build.

Know the Constraint

Stability is found in the boundaries. We build for the "low-capacity version" of yourself, ensuring the system works even when energy is at zero.

Make it Breathable

Interfaces are environments, not just tools. We prioritize negative space and reduced visual noise to prevent cognitive override.

Work the Invisible

Focus on the "Recognition Objects"—the subtle cues that help you observe your own state without the friction of manual tracking.
